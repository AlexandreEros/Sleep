\section{Astrobiological Cognition}\label{sec:astrobiology_cognition}

The principles of the Overfitted Brain Hypothesis, combined with the evidence of convergent evolution (e.g., octopus "active sleep"), suggest that the NREM/REM-like alternation is not a biological quirk, but a fundamental solution to a universal problem. Any complex, plastic nervous system that learns from finite, real-world data will face the problem of overfitting.

Therefore, it is highly plausible that any "sentient" being in the universe, if it learns and adapts, must possess an analogous offline state. This state would serve to consolidate experience (the NREM-like function) and, critically, to regularize and generalize that experience (the REM-like function) to ensure cognitive flexibility.
