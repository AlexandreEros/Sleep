\chapter*{Conclusion}\label{ch:conclusion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{part}{Conclusion}

This report set out to answer a deceptively simple question: \emph{Why do we sleep?} The answer is computational. While the question has not been entirely settled, we postulated that sleep might be a universal, offline learning algorithm; one that appears in jellyfish, octopuses, songbirds, and humans. NREM consolidates; REM generalizes. Dreams are the subjective experience of this process: the ``what-it`s-like'' of a learning brain that must prune, replay, and remix its own data every night.
